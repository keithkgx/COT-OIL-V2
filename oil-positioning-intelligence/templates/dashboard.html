Calculation methodology

Audit definitions used consistently for every market and trader cohort

Net positioning

Long contracts minus short contracts. Spreading shown separately.

Net / open interest

100 × net contracts ÷ total open interest.

Report change

Latest net minus immediately previous net. Not necessarily seven calendar days.

1Y / 3Y percentile

Causal empirical midrank against up to 1/3 years of weekly-equivalent historical readings.

Gross directional

Long + short (does not include separately reported spreading).

Long/short ratio

Long ÷ short; undefined for a zero short position.

Combined reports reflect delta-adjusted futures plus options, not futures alone. COT observations are retrospective and not predictive trading signals. Publication date is not available in the CFTC row-level historical dataset; displayed dates are position dates, not timestamps of public availability.
